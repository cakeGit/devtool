A4 24 round sticker template

A4 portrait sheet, 24 x 40 mm circles, 16 mm side margins, 13.5 mm top/bottom margins, 6 mm gaps. Images export to a 46 mm bleed circle.

Selected sticker: 1

Click a sticker to select it. Drag the image to pan. Use the zoom slider or mouse wheel to zoom. Ctrl+C copies a cell, Ctrl+V pastes a copied cell or a clipboard image, Delete clears the selected cell.
